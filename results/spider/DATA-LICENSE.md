# License of the files in this directory

The `question` and `goldSql` fields, and the `gold-check.*.json` files, reproduce
content of the Spider 1.0 dataset (Yu et al., 2018,
https://yale-lily.github.io/spider), distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These files are therefore
distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well, not under the MIT license of the code.
The only change to the Spider content is its packaging into these files.

The other fields (`reply`, `predSql`, verdicts, latencies, token counts,
`officialExec`, `hardness`) are outputs of this harness and of the models it
evaluated.

Citation:
Tao Yu, Rui Zhang, Kai Yang, Michihiro Yasunaga, Dongxu Wang, Zifan Li, James Ma,
Irene Li, Qingning Yao, Shanelle Roman, Zilin Zhang, Dragomir Radev. "Spider: A
Large-Scale Human-Labeled Dataset for Complex and Cross-Domain Semantic Parsing
and Text-to-SQL Task." EMNLP 2018.
